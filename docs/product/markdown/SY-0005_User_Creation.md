# 1. Purpose & Business Context
Every person who touches the Centuriuum platform — broker, buyer, accountant, bank representative, or company/seller — needs a single account before anything else can happen. This feature defines how that account gets created and how a brand-new user reaches email verification, optional 2FA enrollment, and role selection, whether they arrive through a broker's data room invite or sign up cold from the public website. Getting this right matters because it is the front door for every other module: a broker's deal only starts moving once an accountant or bank contact can log in, and a buyer only sees the marketplace once their account exists. It also has to stay lightweight — asking for too much up front is the fastest way to lose a self-serve signup.
# 2. User Stories
- As a prospective business broker, I want to sign up directly from the public website, so that I can start listing a deal and building my buyer network without waiting on anyone.
- As a business buyer, I want to create an account from the public website, so that I can browse active deal listings that match my interest.
- As an accountant, bank representative, or company/seller invited by a broker, I want to click an invite link and set up my profile quickly, so that I land directly in the deal I was invited to without a generic signup detour.
- As any new user, I want to be told clearly which fields are required and which are optional, so that I'm not discouraged from finishing signup.
- As a broker inviting a party who already has a Centuriuum account, I want that invite to attach to their existing account rather than force a duplicate signup, so that the platform reflects one identity per person across all their deals.
# 3. Functional Requirements
- FR-01: The system shall provide a public sign-up page, reachable without an invite, that collects First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, a Password, and a Role selection (Business Broker, Bank, Business Buyer, Accountant, or Company).
- FR-02: The system shall treat First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Password, and Role as required fields; all other profile fields (title, firm/company name, profile photo, etc., as defined per-role in US - 0001 through US - 0005) shall be optional at signup and completable later.
- FR-03: The system shall support a second entry path — an invite link generated by a broker (per the Onboarding / Invite flow) — that pre-fills and locks the invited email address and carries the inviting broker's ID and the target company/deal ID through to account creation.
- FR-04: The system shall check the invited email address against existing accounts before creating a new one; if a verified account already exists for that email, the system shall attach the new company/deal access grant to the existing account (per SY - 0002) instead of creating a duplicate account, and shall notify the existing user of the new access.
- FR-05: Upon successful signup, the system shall send a welcome/verification email containing a time-limited verification link; the account shall be flagged unverified until that link is used.
- FR-06: The role selected at signup shall be stored as a default label on the account only. It shall not, by itself, grant access to any company or deal; actual per-deal role and permission are determined separately by the broker under SY - 0002 and may differ from the account's default role on different deals.
- FR-07: The system shall prompt every new user, during or immediately after signup, to optionally enroll in two-factor authentication via SMS to the phone number provided. 2FA shall be recommended (with a visible prompt and a brief explanation of why it matters for financial data) but shall not block account creation, email verification, or login if declined.
- FR-08: A user who declines 2FA at signup shall be able to enable it later from account settings.
- FR-09: A user who self-signs up (no invite) and has not yet completed email verification shall be able to log in but shall be restricted from any company/deal data access until verification is complete; the specific restriction on browsing the public buyer marketplace (BY - 0001) while unverified is logged as an Open Question.
- FR-10: Once verified, a self-signed-up user with no company/deal access yet shall land on a role-appropriate default screen: a Business Broker lands on the Deal Tracker / Deal Listing entry point (BR - 0001 / BR - 0003); a Business Buyer lands on the active deal listings view (BY - 0001); an Accountant, Bank, or Company user — roles with no independent deal-sourcing entry point — lands on an account/profile screen indicating that deal access will follow once a broker grants it.
- FR-11: A user who signs up via a broker's invite link shall, upon verification, land directly inside the specific company/deal they were invited to, per the access grant created under SY - 0002, and shall not be routed through the general marketplace/listing landing in FR-10.
- FR-12: The system shall enforce a minimum password policy (minimum 8 characters, at least one letter and one number) at account creation; exact final policy is logged as an Open Question pending Josh's confirmation.
- FR-13: The system shall prevent creation of two accounts with the same email address.
- FR-14: The system shall write account creation, email verification, 2FA enrollment/decline, and role-default-selection events to the Activity & Audit Log (SY - 0003).

# 5. Access & Security
- Roles with access: all roles (Broker, Bank, Business Buyer, Accountant, Company) can self-initiate account creation; no role is restricted from creating an account.
- Roles explicitly excluded: none from account creation itself. However, an unverified account of any role is excluded from all company/deal data until email verification is complete.
- Deal isolation confirmed: this feature is scoped to a single company/deal only. No cross-deal or cross-company visibility of data, documents, or search results. (Account creation itself is deal-agnostic; the moment a user is attached to a specific company/deal via SY - 0002, standard deal isolation applies to everything they see from that point forward.)
# 6. UI / UX Notes
- Platform: Web + Mobile (light). Account creation and profile completion are lightweight forms consistent with the mobile-light scope defined in project conventions; full data-room and role-specific dashboards remain web-only per their own specs.
- Wireframe reference: N/A
The public sign-up form should be a single short screen (name, email, phone, password, role) with optional fields visibly deferred to a "complete your profile later" step, to avoid discouraging self-serve signups. The invite-based path should visually acknowledge the inviting broker and target deal/company (e.g., "You've been invited by [Broker] to [Company/Deal]") so the invited user has context before completing setup. The 2FA prompt should be a single lightweight step (not a hard modal) with a clear skip option.

# 8. Out of Scope / Deferred
- The broker-side mechanics of generating, sending, tracking, and expiring an invite link — that belongs to the Onboarding / Invite flow cross-cutting gap, not this spec.
- Role-specific profile fields, dashboards, and views beyond the common account fields — covered individually in US - 0001 (Broker Profile) through US - 0005 (Company Profile).
- The per-deal role/permission assignment mechanism itself — covered in SY - 0002 (Company Access Setup).
- Password reset / forgot-password flow — standard account-recovery mechanics, not specific to user creation; to be covered in its own spec if not already assumed as platform infrastructure.
- Buyer qualification/KYC (proof of funds, verification grading) — covered separately in BY - 0007.
# 9. Open Questions
- Exact password complexity policy: is the minimum-8-characters-plus-letter-and-number assumption in FR-12 sufficient, or should Centuriuum require a stronger policy (e.g., special character, longer minimum) at launch?
- Should an unverified self-signed-up Business Buyer be allowed to browse the public deal listing marketplace (BY - 0001) before verifying their email, or should marketplace browsing itself require verification?
- Should email verification be a hard gate on login itself (no login at all until verified), or only a gate on deal/company data access as currently assumed in FR-09?
- Once the Onboarding / Invite flow gap is specced, does the invite link expire, and if so, what happens to an account created after expiration — does the deal/company access grant still attach, or does the invited user land as a plain self-signup?
- Should Accountant, Bank, and Company users who self-sign up (no invite) be able to search for or request a connection to a specific broker/deal from their holding screen (FR-10), or is a broker-initiated invite the only path for those roles?
# 10. Acceptance Criteria
- A new user can complete self-service signup from the public site with only First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Password, and Role, and no other field blocks account creation.
- A welcome/verification email is sent immediately on signup, and the account is flagged unverified until the link is used.
- A user can decline 2FA at signup without being blocked, and can enable it later from account settings.
- An invite-link signup pre-fills and locks the invited email, and on verification lands the user directly inside the correct company/deal rather than a generic marketplace screen.
- Inviting an email address that already has a verified account attaches the new deal access to that existing account instead of creating a duplicate.
- Changing a user's per-deal role in SY - 0002 does not alter their account-level default role from signup.
- Account creation, verification, and 2FA enrollment/decline events all appear in the Activity & Audit Log.
